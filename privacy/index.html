Privacy Notice

How this website handles inquiries and scheduling.

Information you choose to send

If you email or call Stratum, we receive the information you provide and use it to respond to your inquiry. Do not send tax returns or other sensitive records by ordinary email.

Consultation links on this site take you to AE Tax Advisors. Information you enter into AE's booking process is handled by AE and its scheduling providers. Review AE Tax Advisors' privacy notice before submitting information there.

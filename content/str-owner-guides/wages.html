Rental property depreciation does not automatically offset W-2 wages. Before describing a cost segregation estimate as wage-income savings, ask your preparer to determine the activity’s treatment, applicable loss limitations and how much of the deduction can be used. A large component allocation is not enough to answer those questions.

Begin with the loss, not a promised refund

Separate rental income, operating items, depreciation and other tax adjustments in the preparer’s model. Compare depreciation with and without the study. Then evaluate the incremental amount under the owner’s actual limitations. An illustration that skips those steps may show a tax reduction the owner cannot claim in the modeled year.

Ask which rules apply to your activity

Publication 925 describes passive-activity restrictions, rental exceptions, material participation and a special rental-real-estate allowance subject to conditions and phaseouts. There are not simply “exactly two routes” for every wage earner. Real estate professional status, where relevant, also requires a separate participation analysis for the affected rental activity.

For an STR, a short customer-use period can be relevant to the rental-activity exception, but it does not alone establish a deductible nonpassive loss. Have the preparer apply the rules rather than treating self-management or a platform listing as automatic qualification.

Compare usable benefit with the complete cost

Stratum studies cost $1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per property. Include separately engaged filing and advisory work in the decision. Ask when a limited deduction might become usable, what assumptions could change and how a future sale affects the comparison.

Do not assume the same marginal rate applies to an entire preliminary deduction. A preparer-reviewed tax projection can evaluate the relevant income, limitations and timing. The study economics guide explains the decision inputs without promising a wage offset.

Before a return is implemented

Obtain the preparer’s conclusion, the accepted asset schedule and a clear assignment of filing responsibilities. Resolve changes to the property or activity before relying on an earlier projection. Review the report handoff checklist and when to wait if a material question remains unanswered.

A section 481(a) adjustment can be part of an accounting-method change. Its purpose is to account for amounts affected by the change without duplicating or omitting them. It is not an automatic bonus deduction available whenever an owner orders a cost segregation study.

Resolve the procedural route first

The preparer must identify the existing and proposed methods, the taxpayer and asset eligibility, and the applicable consent procedure. Some changes use a cut-off method rather than a section 481 adjustment. A correction or election may require a different route. Start with the Form 3115 guide for eligibility and filing responsibility; this page focuses on the calculation handoff.

What the reconciliation should contain

  • Approved original basis and the nondepreciable land allocation.
  • Asset-by-asset service dates and prior depreciation treatment.
  • Proposed classifications and the historical rules supporting them.
  • Prior elections, improvements, disposals and earlier method changes.
  • A bridge between the former treatment, proposed treatment and net adjustment.

The study date does not reset acquisition dates. Bonus eligibility, elections, conventions and other rules relevant to the historical assets must be considered; applying today's percentage to every older cost is not a sound reconstruction.

Sign and timing matter

The Form 3115 instructions generally describe a one-year adjustment period for a negative adjustment and four years for a positive adjustment, with exceptions. Do not assume every study yields a favorable adjustment, or that every positive adjustment follows the same period. The preparer should cite the procedure and explain the schedule used.

Illustrative arithmetic only: if supported cumulative depreciation under the proposed method is $120,000 and the comparable former-method amount is $80,000, the difference is $40,000. That comparison alone does not establish eligibility, the filed adjustment or the owner's tax saving.

An adjustment is separate from usable loss

A deduction can remain subject to basis, at-risk, passive activity, excess business loss and other applicable rules. Cash distributions from a partnership are not automatically taxable business income available to absorb it. Review passive loss treatment and the excess business loss review before projecting a refund.

STR owner handoff

Provide furnishing purchases, rental-use changes and removed assets alongside the old schedules. Assign responsibility for the calculation, filing and revised ledger in writing. The existing-property checklist helps gather the history; the report handoff connects that history to implementation.

An inherited rental property may need a new basis analysis before a cost segregation study. Do not assume every inheritance receives a full upward adjustment, that the old owner's entire depreciation schedule simply continues, or that every newly allocated component qualifies for bonus depreciation.

Confirm what was inherited

Publication 551 generally describes inherited basis using date-of-death fair market value or an applicable alternative, with exceptions and consistency requirements. The result can be lower as well as higher than earlier basis. Joint ownership, trusts, partial interests and entity-held property require attention to what interest actually changed hands.

An inherited partnership interest is not automatically the same as a direct inheritance of every asset the partnership owns. Have the estate and tax advisers resolve the relevant owner and property-level basis before sending a value to the study team.

Gather the basis evidence

  • Estate or trust documents and ownership records.
  • Relevant appraisal and valuation date.
  • Estate-tax value reporting, including Form 8971 information if applicable.
  • Allocation among land, building, furnishings and other property.
  • Post-inheritance improvements, rental-use facts and current schedules.

The land allocation guide helps organize the split. An appraisal total alone does not make land depreciable or establish the recovery period of every amenity.

Review depreciation eligibility separately

Inherited property is not purchased property for every expensing rule. Publication 946 states that property acquired by inheritance does not qualify for section 179. Bonus eligibility also has its own acquisition and basis requirements; do not promise a first-year bonus deduction just because a study identifies shorter-life components. Later assets the heir purchases require their own review.

Distinguish the decedent's returns from the heir's returns

The study cannot simply claim all depreciation the former owner might have taken. Establish the heir's basis, applicable method and service history. If the heir has already filed returns using a treatment that may need changing, ask the preparer about the applicable correction or method-change procedure. Inherited status alone does not establish a permanent ban on Form 3115.

Use the filing procedure guide only after that ownership and basis review, and retain the existing-property history.

When the inherited property is an Airbnb

Document personal stays, rental availability, transferred furniture and new purchases. A decision to operate a vacation rental does not itself establish nonpassive losses or a study's economic benefit. Review vacation-rental use and loss limitations before projecting savings. Confirm scope and implementation costs before commissioning work.

Real estate professional status can change how rental real estate activities are treated under the passive activity rules. It does not by itself make every rental loss deductible, and a cost segregation study does not establish the owner’s status.

Two annual qualification tests

Publication 925 describes a qualifying taxpayer as performing more than 750 hours of services during the year in real property trades or businesses in which the taxpayer materially participates, and performing more than half of their personal services in those businesses. Both tests require a facts-based annual review. A job title, license or property count does not replace them.

For a joint return, one spouse must separately satisfy the qualification requirements; the spouses cannot simply combine their hours to pass those two tests. Employee work has an additional ownership rule, addressed in Publication 925. Give the preparer the full work history before excluding or counting those hours.

Qualification and rental participation are separate

A qualifying real estate professional must still address material participation in the rental activity for nonpassive treatment. An election to treat rental real estate interests as a single activity may be available, but it is not automatically required or automatically beneficial. Discuss its continuing consequences and the property portfolio before making it.

Short-stay activity can require a different starting analysis under the rental-activity exceptions. Do not assume an Airbnb owner must qualify as a real estate professional, or that short stays alone establish nonpassive treatment. See the STR activity review and the comparative participation test.

Prepare evidence that explains the work

  • Separate services by person, date, activity and the work actually performed.
  • Keep calendars, communications, invoices and management records that support reasonable time estimates.
  • Identify employee, investor and property-management roles for review rather than counting every real-estate-related hour.
  • Account for other employment and business work when reviewing the more-than-half test.
  • Give the preparer spouse and outside-manager participation facts relevant to the applicable tests.

Publication 925 permits proof of participation by reasonable means; a particular daily log format is not universally mandated. A reliable contemporaneous record is useful, but a template alone does not establish eligibility.

Nonpassive does not mean unlimited

Basis, at-risk, excess business loss and other applicable limitations may still affect deductions. State treatment may differ. Ask the preparer to show how the study’s depreciation reaches the owner’s return after the relevant limits, rather than multiplying the entire proposed deduction by a tax bracket.

Coordinate before commissioning a study

Use the loss limitation guide to identify unanswered questions. Review the study economics using realistic usable benefit and implementation costs. A supported classification report and a supported participation conclusion are separate pieces of the decision.

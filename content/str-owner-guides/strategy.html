The phrase “STR tax loophole” is often used for several separate tax questions. A cost segregation report addresses property components and costs. Whether a resulting loss is currently deductible depends on the owner’s facts and the applicable limitations. Buying an Airbnb and ordering a study does not, by itself, establish a wage-income offset.

Keep four questions separate

  1. Depreciable investment: what supported basis and assets belong to this owner?
  2. Activity treatment: how do the actual customer-use periods and services affect the passive-activity analysis?
  3. Participation: which test, if any, is satisfied by qualifying work and reliable records?
  4. Loss usability: what other limitations, elections and state adjustments apply?

Ask your preparer to document the conclusion for each question. A study estimate cannot substitute for an unresolved answer elsewhere in that sequence.

What the short-stay exception does and does not establish

Publication 925 describes an exception from rental-activity treatment where average customer use is seven days or less. That is not an automatic nonpassive-loss rule. Participation and other relevant requirements still need review. Do not infer the result from the average booking length shown on a dashboard without confirming the records and calculation.

The comparative material-participation test requires more than 100 hours and participation at least as great as any other individual. It is one test among several. Review examples and evidence questions for that test, including the manager’s role.

Give the property analysis its own evidence file

Gather purchase records, supported land allocation, seller-transferred furnishings, later invoices and earlier depreciation schedules. Keep each improvement event separate. Ask the study team to explain component costs and classifications rather than promising that a fixed share of every rental will move into short-life categories.

A report completed this year does not reset an older asset’s acquisition history. Use the bonus-depreciation checklist and have the preparer determine the implementation procedure for earlier depreciation.

Model the result before committing

Request a comparison with and without the study that identifies assumptions, usable deductions, fees and future consequences. Stratum studies cost $1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per property. Add separately engaged implementation costs. A claimed large first-year deduction is not the same as a supported reduction in current tax.

For example, if the preparer has not yet determined participation or loss usability, record the benefit as unresolved. Do not fill that gap by multiplying the study’s preliminary deduction by the owner’s highest tax rate. The fee-versus-assumed-benefit worksheet makes assumptions visible but does not determine eligibility.

Leave a reviewable decision trail

Keep the property file, activity records, written scope, preparer conclusions and accepted report version connected. Ask who will implement the result and what evidence will show that implementation is complete. Continue with the document checklist and proposal normalization.

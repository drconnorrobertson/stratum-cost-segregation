A federal cost segregation deduction does not establish the same state deduction. State law, taxpayer type, filing year and property facts must be reviewed separately. A national list labeled conforming or nonconforming can obscure exceptions and become stale after legislation changes.

Start with the returns actually required

Identify the property's location, owner or entity, resident state and relevant state filings. A state with no individual income tax does not eliminate every entity-level tax or another state's reporting obligations. Ask the preparer which returns are affected before estimating the combined benefit.

Use year-specific state instructions

California's 2025 Form FTB 3885A instructions explain federal and California depreciation differences, including treatment of the federal special depreciation allowance. New York's 2025 Form IT-398 addresses section 168(k) property and exceptions. These are examples of official filing-year guidance, not a complete 2026 state survey or instructions for every entity type.

For a 2026 projection, confirm enacted law and available 2026 guidance before carrying forward a 2025 conclusion. A general rolling conformity description does not override a state's specific exception. Use the state tax agency's instructions for the return and asset year involved.

Do not assume every state recovers an add-back over the same number of years, uses the same convention, or ultimately produces an equal tax saving. The governing rules and future income determine the result.

For STR owners operating across states

Keep one property and asset identifier across the study, federal schedule and each applicable state schedule. Track personal-use changes, renovations and replaced furnishings so an adjustment is not lost when a property changes managers or preparers. Use the CPA handoff guide to assign responsibility for those schedules.

Measure benefit after the state review

Ask for separate federal and state timing estimates, implementation fees and carryover assumptions. Do not multiply the proposed federal deduction by a combined tax rate unless the preparer supports both treatments. Review the ROI checklist, convention review and loss limitations together before committing to a study.

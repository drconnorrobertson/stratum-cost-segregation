What the study and CPA review each address

The property work should connect the analyzed cost pool to identifiable components, supporting records and explained classifications. Your tax preparer separately evaluates the owner’s activity, participation, elections and loss limitations. An Airbnb listing alone does not establish a deduction against wages.

A furnished rental may have purchased furniture, installed equipment, site work and building systems. Their names alone do not determine recovery periods. Ownership, use, installation facts and the applicable depreciation rules matter. Review the STR furnishings guide before supplying a lump-sum furnishing budget.

What to expect from intake to implementation

  1. Discovery: describe the property, size, acquisition history, use and current CPA relationship.
  2. Scope: confirm included work, fee, records, inspection approach and delivery dependencies in writing.
  3. Property analysis: reconcile supported costs and document the component conclusions and limitations.
  4. Preparer handoff: agree who resolves questions, implements schedules and evaluates any filing or accounting-method work.

Ask for the property-specific delivery expectation after the records are reviewed. A report deadline, a return deadline and an election deadline may be different. Do not infer a guaranteed turnaround from a generic example.

Will an STR study produce a usable benefit?

Compare the incremental deduction your preparer expects you can use with the fee, implementation costs and holding-period consequences. A study may be less compelling when records are incomplete, deductions cannot be used soon, or a sale is planned. Work through the study decision and when to wait or decline.

For bonus depreciation, use the acquisition and service-date rules applicable to the assets. Current federal rules include restored 100% bonus depreciation for certain qualifying property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025; that is not an automatic deduction for the whole house. Review the dated bonus-depreciation checklist with your preparer.

Prepare for a useful first conversation

Have your property address, approximate square footage, closing statement, land-allocation support, furnishing records and existing depreciation schedule available. Identify personal-use periods, later renovations and any missing documents. Use the readiness tool or the detailed document checklist. Arrange secure document sharing with the engaged professionals.

For an STR or Airbnb, furniture and equipment records should explain what you own, how the item is used, its supported cost and when it became available for that use. A room label or the abbreviation FF&E does not establish a recovery period, bonus eligibility or immediate deduction.

Furniture is not automatically seven-year property

IRS Publication 527 places appliances, carpeting and furniture used in a residential rental real estate activity in the five-year GDS class. It separately lists office furniture as seven-year property. Confirm the activity, applicable asset class and depreciation system for your situation rather than assigning every sofa, bed or table a seven-year period.

A furnished STR also does not automatically establish a 27.5-year building recovery period. Ask the preparer to assess the building’s actual use and relevant definitions. Keep the component classification question separate from the passive-activity treatment of the owner’s rental operation.

Describe installations before labeling assets

For a hot tub, describe the unit, pad, enclosure, connections and ownership separately. For smart-home equipment, identify the devices, wiring and installation invoice. For cabinets or lighting, give the reviewer photographs and the actual function, not a preferred tax label. A pool, spa or outdoor kitchen is not automatically one short-life asset because guests use it.

On replacements, identify the old asset and schedule entry as well as the new invoice. Give the preparer the removal facts before treating remaining basis as a deductible loss. Keep the improvement timeline connected to the asset record.

Consider expensing rules without assuming eligibility

The de minimis safe harbor has conditions beyond an item’s price. IRS guidance describes a $2,500 threshold for taxpayers without an applicable financial statement and $5,000 with one, generally per invoice or substantiated item. Without an AFS, written procedures are not required, but a consistent beginning-of-year accounting policy and book treatment matter; an AFS taxpayer needs written procedures. Ask the preparer about the annual election and requirements.

Separately confirm bonus eligibility, dates and elections. A cost segregation study does not mean all furniture is deductible in full this year, and an expense election does not eliminate the need to reconcile the purchase records.

Hand off the inventory with the building study

Agree which items are included in the study and which your preparer handles separately. Use matching identifiers so the report and asset schedule can be reconciled. Keep the final version, excluded items and unresolved questions together. Review the report acceptance checklist before implementation.

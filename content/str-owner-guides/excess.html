Passing a material participation test does not establish that an entire STR loss can offset wages this year. Noncorporate taxpayers may also need to apply the excess business loss limitation under section 461(l). Review the sequence of applicable limits before treating accelerated depreciation as usable cash savings.

Use the threshold for the correct tax year

Revenue Procedure 2025-32 sets the excess business loss threshold for tax years beginning in 2026 at $256,000, or $512,000 for joint returns. The 2025 Form 461 instructions use different 2025 amounts. A return filed during 2026 is not necessarily a 2026 tax-year return.

The threshold is part of a taxpayer-level calculation of aggregate trade or business income and deductions, with specific inclusion and exclusion rules. It is not a cap on the property's depreciation deduction in isolation. Wages are not treated as trade or business income for this calculation merely because they appear on the same return.

Review other limitations first

The Form 461 instructions address applying other loss limits before this calculation. A preparer may need to review owner basis, amounts at risk and passive activity treatment before determining what reaches Form 461. See the passive loss guide and real estate professional review.

A transparent illustration

Assume a joint-return taxpayer has a $720,000 net trade or business loss included in the 2026 calculation, no other relevant business items, and has cleared other applicable restrictions. Against the $512,000 threshold, the simplified excess is $208,000. These are hypothetical inputs, not a study allocation, an Airbnb eligibility conclusion or a complete return calculation.

Do not call $780,000 a net loss and then subtract rental receipts again. A projection must label gross receipts, expenses and the resulting net loss consistently before applying any limit.

Track the NOL handoff separately

The disallowed excess business loss is treated as a net operating loss carryover to the following year under the applicable rules. Later use depends on the NOL provisions and future facts. Do not guarantee that the full amount will offset the next year's income, or that deferral has no economic cost.

Cost segregation and a section 1031 exchange answer different questions. A study supports the allocation of property costs for depreciation. An exchange may defer gain on qualifying real property held for investment or business use. Neither establishes the other’s tax treatment.

Start the review before the STR sale closes

A furnished Airbnb can involve a building, land, furniture, equipment and later improvements. The transaction documents and depreciation schedules need to identify what is being transferred. Do not assume everything sold with the property qualifies for exchange treatment merely because it appeared in the same purchase agreement.

  • Give your adviser the original closing statement, study, depreciation schedules and improvement history.
  • Separate proposed allocations for real property and furnishings, with support for the amounts.
  • Discuss investment use, personal use, the exchange structure and timing with your tax adviser and qualified intermediary before closing.
  • Model any recognized gain and recapture alongside selling costs and replacement-property cash needs.

An exchange does not erase the depreciation history

Publication 544 explains that like-kind exchange treatment is limited to qualifying real property; other property or money can produce recognized gain. Its recapture rules require a separate review. A statement that an exchange always defers all depreciation recapture is too broad. Section 1245 ordinary-income recapture and section 1250 treatment are not a single universal 25% tax charge.

Review the depreciation recapture guide with the actual asset schedules. The sales allocation, depreciation previously allowed or allowable and property received all matter. An accelerated deduction today should be evaluated with the expected holding period and exit.

Replacement property needs its own basis reconciliation

Do not restart depreciation using the full replacement purchase price without an exchange calculation. The preparer must reconcile the carried-over basis and any additional basis under the applicable exchange and depreciation rules. Additional cash invested is not automatically taxable boot received. A new study must work with that reconciliation rather than treating the exchange as an entirely new cash purchase.

Owner handoff: request a schedule connecting each relinquished asset, its adjusted basis, sale allocation, exchange treatment and replacement depreciation input. Keep the exchange documents with the original study and subsequent reports.

For Airbnb and vacation-rental owners

Personal-use history can affect whether the property qualifies for the intended treatment. A booking calendar alone does not establish investment-use eligibility. Gather owner stays, rental availability, leases and operating records for both properties. See the vacation-rental use review and furnishing evidence guide.

Decide whether a study still fits the transaction

Ask the preparer to compare the usable timing benefit with the study fee, implementation cost and exit consequences before commissioning work close to a sale. If the exchange facts or basis records remain unresolved, the reasons to pause a study are a useful next step.

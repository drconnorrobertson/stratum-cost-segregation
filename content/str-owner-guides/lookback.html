A look-back cost segregation study examines property already placed in service. Form 3115 is an application to change an accounting method. A study can supply classification and cost evidence, but it does not establish that a particular owner qualifies for a method change or a current deduction.

First determine what needs to change

Ask the preparer whether the issue is an accounting method, a correctable error, a first-year treatment or an election requiring its own procedure. The number and nature of prior returns, previous changes, examination status and the applicable guidance can affect the available route. Do not assume that an amended return is always required, or that it is never appropriate.

What a section 481 adjustment does

When applicable, a section 481(a) adjustment accounts for the effect of changing methods so amounts are not duplicated or omitted. Its calculation must reconcile the former treatment with the proposed method. It is not simply a percentage of the property’s current market value.

The sign and applicable procedure affect adjustment timing. Even a supported negative adjustment does not establish that the owner can use the resulting loss against wages or other income. Review passive loss limitations and other applicable limits separately.

A study date does not reset historic tax facts

Reconstruct original acquisition costs, land allocation, placed-in-service dates, later improvements, prior depreciation and elections. Apply the rules relevant to those facts. A report prepared now does not turn an older acquisition into newly acquired property or automatically apply this year’s bonus treatment.

Agree on filing responsibility before ordering work

  1. Ask the tax preparer to identify the proposed change and applicable consent procedure.
  2. Confirm eligibility and the required attachments, calculations, deadlines and filing destinations using current instructions and applicable procedural guidance.
  3. Give the study team the complete prior schedules and known disposals.
  4. Reconcile the final component schedule to the existing tax ledger and proposed adjustment.
  5. Retain the filed application and evidence that required filing steps were completed.

Scope question: confirm in writing who prepares, signs, files and supports Form 3115. Study preparation and tax-return implementation are separate responsibilities unless the engagement expressly includes both.

For an STR operated over several years

Gather owner-use history, changes between short- and long-term rental, furnishing purchases, renovations and replaced assets. Missing history can change the proposed calculation and the time needed to implement it. Start with the existing-property records review, then use the report acceptance checklist to connect the study to the preparer’s work.

Evaluate the full economics

Compare expected usable timing benefits with the study fee, filing cost, state treatment and expected sale. Longer ownership does not automatically mean a larger worthwhile deduction. If the method or loss-usability decision is unresolved, pause the projection rather than promising a catch-up tax saving.

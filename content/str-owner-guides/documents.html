A useful STR cost segregation document checklist connects each number to a property, event and retrievable source. Collect the original evidence before converting it into a spreadsheet. An organized file can reveal missing facts early; it does not establish tax eligibility or guarantee that the study can proceed.

Start with a property cover sheet

Record the address, parcel or unit identifier, ownership entity, approximate square footage and the person authorized to answer questions. Add the acquisition date, intended rental use and existing CPA contact. An LLC name, property-manager nickname and Airbnb listing name may refer to the same home; document that connection.

Keep the cost bridge separate from payments

One contractor invoice can be paid in several transfers. A card statement can include purchases for several properties. Link payments to final invoices and credits instead of adding every amount to the cost pool. Label estimates and unresolved differences so the reviewer can distinguish them from supported costs.

For a furnished acquisition, keep seller-transferred inventory separate from later replacements. For a condo, identify unit-owned work and common property. A property-listing photograph is useful context, but not proof of cost or ownership. Follow the seller-inventory guide for the acquisition file.

Build a chronology without guessing dates

Record purchase, project completion, rental readiness and later changes separately. A booking calendar and a contractor invoice may establish different events. Supply the evidence and let the preparer determine which date controls. If a date is reconstructed, preserve the supporting explanation rather than replacing uncertainty with a convenient entry.

When a document is missing

  1. Name the missing fact, not just the missing file.
  2. Ask who can supply the original record or a suitable alternative.
  3. Record the follow-up owner and expected response.
  4. Ask the study provider whether the gap changes scope, timing or a report limitation.
  5. Keep the final resolution with the original request.

For manager-controlled records, use the STR manager handoff guide. Do not invent contractor detail or allocate a lump sum solely to achieve a preferred result.

Agree on the final handoff

Confirm the report format, schedule identifiers, CPA contact and process for correcting errors. Keep the accepted report version and open-item list together. Arrange secure document transfer with the engaged professionals; this public guide and its download do not collect or submit your records.

How to Choose the Best STR Cost Seg Firm

Published September 27, 2026 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Stratum Cost Segregation · Editorial policy

The best STR cost seg firm for your short-term rental should explain its property analysis, document the costs behind its report, give you a written fee and scope, and coordinate a usable handoff to your CPA. Start with those four requirements when comparing cost segregation companies for an Airbnb, furnished vacation home, cabin or small rental portfolio.

Compare the work product before the projected deduction

Request proposals using the same property file. Include the closing statement, supported land allocation, furnishing inventory, improvement history, rental-use dates and existing depreciation schedule. Ask each firm to mark missing information instead of filling it with an unexplained assumption. A higher proposed deduction can reflect different inputs rather than better work.

What an STR proposal needs beyond a standard property quote

Furnishings purchased with the home versus later purchases

A furnished cabin purchase and a later furniture refresh are separate events. Supply the seller inventory, purchase agreement and later invoices. Ask how the study reconciles transferred items with costs already on your asset schedule. Room photographs help identify items, but do not prove what you paid or who owned them.

Opening dates, renovations and personal use

Prepare a chronology of acquisition, repairs, furnishing, rental readiness and later improvements. Publication 527 describes placed-in-service timing in terms of readiness and availability for the intended rental use. A booking date alone may not establish that date. Keep personal-use records and work completed after opening separate for preparer review.

Study conclusions versus the owner’s ability to use losses

Publication 925 includes an exception from rental-activity treatment when average customer use is seven days or less. That exception alone does not establish material participation or a deductible loss against wages. Your preparer must evaluate the relevant activity, participation, loss limitations and state treatment. A study provider’s component analysis does not replace that review.

One property versus several

For several rentals, ask for a separate property identifier, cost reconciliation and service-date history for each. Confirm whether the fee minimum applies per property and whether sampling is proposed. Start with the portfolio preparation tool to organize record readiness; it does not rank tax benefits.

Compare fees with a benefit your preparer has evaluated

Stratum studies cost $1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per property. For illustration, 1,500 square feet gives a $2,000 study fee; 2,500 square feet gives $2,500; two separate 1,500-square-foot properties give $4,000 in combined study fees. These are fee calculations, not client outcomes or tax-savings estimates. Confirm study scope and separately engaged tax or filing services during discovery.

Compare that cost with the incremental deduction your preparer expects you can use, implementation costs, future depreciation, state adjustments and an anticipated sale. A study can accelerate deductions without creating permanent savings. Use the fee-versus-assumed-benefit worksheet with adviser-supported inputs, and review current Stratum pricing.

Three situations to discuss before choosing a firm

  • New furnished Airbnb: establish which furnishings transferred, what you bought later and when the property became ready for rental use. Request an example of the proposed basis reconciliation.
  • Existing rental with prior returns: supply the actual depreciation schedules. Ask your preparer to determine the implementation procedure and filing work; do not assume the study fee includes it.
  • Renovated vacation home with owner stays: separate original acquisition, later projects and personal use. Ask which facts must be resolved before the firm will finalize the report.

Questions about choosing the best STR cost seg firm

Which firm is best for an Airbnb or vacation rental?

Choose against your property, record quality and required support. Compare the actual engagement, technical responsibility, component evidence, total cost and preparer handoff. An affiliate list, award badge or large deduction example is not a substitute for a relevant sample report and written scope.

Should I choose the cheapest STR study?

Price is one factor. First establish whether the quotes cover comparable evidence collection, analysis, deliverables and support. A small fee difference matters less if a missing deliverable requires additional professional work. A higher price also does not independently prove quality.

Does an STR study guarantee a deduction against W-2 income?

No. The physical-property analysis and the owner’s tax treatment are separate reviews. Ask your preparer to evaluate the activity and participation facts before relying on a projected deduction.

Is an “IRS-approved” firm a meaningful selection criterion?

The cited IRS audit guide is not a provider certification. Ask for the underlying evidence, methodology and written support terms. Do not interpret a firm’s reference to IRS guidance as a guarantee that a report or return cannot be examined.

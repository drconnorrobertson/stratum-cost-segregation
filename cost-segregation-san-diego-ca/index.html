STRO categories and room-versus-whole-home use

San Diego’s STRO page distinguishes several hosting categories, including home sharing and whole-home use. Match the licensing and use facts to the particular unit before making a study request; other San Diego County cities have their own jurisdictional context. San Diego short-term residential occupancy program.

Define the property study

For a home-sharing arrangement, identify guest-only rooms, shared living areas, and owner-only space. For a whole-home rental, reconcile any personal-use periods and later changes in operation. The component inventory should describe dedicated rental furnishings separately from shared building assets. An operating category informs the fact file but does not determine federal depreciation treatment by itself.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • Applicable STRO category and unit identifier
  • Guest/owner use map and rental calendar
  • Dedicated furnishing receipts plus shared-improvement scopes

A scoping example

A homeowner rents rooms for part of the year and later offers the entire home while away. Keep a dated operating timeline and identify any furnishings added for the changed use. Do not assume that one year-end photograph accurately describes rental use throughout the year.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Does an STRO tier settle the federal tax classification?

No. City licensing and federal tax rules answer different questions. Provide the operating facts and use history so the preparer can evaluate the appropriate tax treatment separately.

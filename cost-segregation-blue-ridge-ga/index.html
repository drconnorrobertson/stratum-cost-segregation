County lodging records and cabin site-work packages

Fannin County’s lodging-tax page links its STR materials. Determine whether the cabin falls under the county or a city authority before using that resource for the operating file; the Blue Ridge destination label alone is not enough. Fannin County lodging-tax and STR resources.

Define the property study

For a cabin with access-road work, retaining improvements, a deck, and a hot tub, identify the purpose of each project. Site contractors may combine grading that supports the building with other improvements. Request cost detail and photographs showing where the work occurred. Keep maintenance spending and capital-project records separate for the preparer’s review before the study team receives its scope.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • Parcel jurisdiction and lodging-account file
  • Access, grading, retaining, and deck construction scopes
  • Hot-tub and equipment invoices tied to installation dates

A scoping example

A single excavation invoice covers a building pad and a guest parking area. Ask for quantities or other factual detail supporting the split. A generic site-improvement percentage would hide the different functions that the study needs to evaluate.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Can all grading costs be called land improvements?

Not automatically. Explain what the work supports and how the costs were developed. Land, building-support work, and other improvements need fact-specific treatment rather than a label chosen from the invoice title.

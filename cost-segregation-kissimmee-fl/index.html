County overlay checks and themed-room invoices

Osceola County publishes information about STR planned-development zoning and its overlay map. Confirm whether county or city jurisdiction governs the specific Kissimmee-area parcel before relying on that map for an operating decision. Osceola County STR planned-development information.

Define the property study

Themed rooms can combine freestanding beds, electronics, specialty lighting, permanent framing, and custom millwork. Obtain the designer’s procurement list and the builder’s installation detail. A single invoice for a themed bedroom can hide costs already included in the property purchase or items owned by a resort association. Reconcile the package to the owner’s ledger before the study begins.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • Jurisdiction and applicable zoning confirmation
  • Designer item list with separate materials and installation scopes
  • Seller furnishings and newly purchased items reconciled by room

A scoping example

A room package includes a freestanding bunk bed and a permanently constructed play feature. Photos show the final theme, but invoices need to explain attachment, function, and cost. The study provider should analyze those components separately rather than classify the theme as one asset.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Does calling a room furniture make all of its construction furniture?

No. Vendor marketing categories do not replace component analysis. Ask for enough detail to distinguish movable contents from structural and installed work before proposing a recovery classification.

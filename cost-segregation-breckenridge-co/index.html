STR Cost Segregation in Breckenridge, CO

Breckenridge STR cost segregation: license zones and association-managed mountain units. Review local resources, study records, and a property-specific planning example.

Stratum Cost Segregation · Updated September 27, 2026

License zones and association-managed mountain units

Breckenridge publishes accommodation-license information, including caps and zones. Check the applicable zone and current licensing position directly with the town before using expected STR operation as an assumption in a study feasibility discussion. Breckenridge accommodation-unit licenses.

Define the property study

For a resort condo, obtain the unit deed, association documents, and management contract. Identify whether a furnishing refresh was purchased by the owner, funded through an assessment, or retained by an operator. Those arrangements can change the records needed to define the study. Keep the operating license and management agreement separate from the component-cost evidence.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • Address and applicable accommodation-license category
  • Unit ownership documents and management agreement
  • Furniture-refresh invoices identifying purchaser and owner

A scoping example

A rental manager replaces a furnishing package and deducts the cost from owner distributions. Obtain the underlying vendor detail and reconcile the deduction to the owner ledger. A net distribution statement alone does not explain what assets were acquired or which unit received them.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Can my manager’s annual statement be the full asset file?

Usually it needs supporting detail. Ask for invoices, inventory changes, and project dates that reconcile to the statement. The study provider should be able to identify the actual items behind capital charges.

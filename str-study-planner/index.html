Prepare Your STR Cost Segregation Study

Build a tailored cost segregation document checklist for furnished rentals, condos, renovations, personal use, managers, and property portfolios.

Build a document checklist for your property. The choices below organize a study discussion; they do not determine tax eligibility or estimate a deduction. No address, income, or account information is requested.

Which property facts apply?

6 starting records to gather.

Your study preparation checklist

  • Closing statement and executed purchase agreement
  • Supported land and building basis information for preparer review
  • Property identity and actual ownership interest
  • Timeline of acquisition and availability for rental use
  • Current photographs or available plans
  • Questions about study scope, fee, deliverables, and CPA coordination
View all scenario-specific records

Furnished purchase

  • Executed seller inventory and bill of sale
  • Closing credits and excluded items
  • Post-closing furniture receipts and replacement log

Condo or shared property

  • Deed and condominium declaration
  • Association assessment notices and project descriptions
  • Owner-versus-association asset inventory

Renovations

  • Final contractor scope and approved change orders
  • Payment reconciliation including deposits and credits
  • Improvement completion dates and replaced-asset records

Personal or mixed use

  • Floor plan of guest-only, owner-only, and shared space
  • Rental and personal-use calendar
  • Conversion history and basis questions for the preparer

Manager records

  • Vendor invoices underlying capital charges
  • Management agreement and asset-ownership explanation
  • Manager inventory and rental-availability timeline

Multiple properties

  • Entity-to-property identifier map
  • Separate basis and acquisition file for each property
  • Bulk purchase allocations and furniture-transfer register

Previously depreciated property

  • Latest depreciation and fixed-asset schedules
  • Prior study, if any, and implementation records
  • List of assets retained, removed, replaced, or transferred

Download blank working templates

  • Furnished-property inventory (CSV)
  • Improvement and date reconciliation (CSV)
  • Portfolio record index (CSV)

These are organizing worksheets, not completed valuations or tax schedules. Fill them privately and use your adviser’s secure document-transfer process.

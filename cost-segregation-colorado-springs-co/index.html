Keep the new owner’s approval and the seller’s property history separate

Colorado Springs states that an STR permit is issued to a specific owner rather than running with the property. Confirm your own operating status when acquiring an existing rental. Colorado Springs short-term rentals.

Define the property study

A going-concern sale can include reservation history, furnishings, and recent seller improvements. For a property study, reconcile what the buyer acquired under the contract rather than simply importing the seller’s depreciation schedule. Preserve old records as descriptive evidence and clearly distinguish them from the buyer’s cost documentation.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • Buyer closing statement and executed asset schedule
  • Seller inventory verified at possession
  • New owner’s permit file and post-closing improvement receipts

A scoping example

A seller supplies a depreciation report from an earlier acquisition. The buyer pays a different price and excludes several furniture items from the sale. Give the provider both the historical report and the current contract, with the excluded items identified.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Can I reuse the seller’s study without adjustment?

Ask the provider and return preparer to evaluate it. The prior report may help describe components, but it does not establish your transaction costs or automatically become your depreciation schedule.

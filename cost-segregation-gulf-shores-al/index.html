Gulf Shores STR cost segregation: separate property files for separately licensed rentals. Review local resources, study records, and a property-specific planning example.

Separate property files for separately licensed rentals

Gulf Shores directs rental owners to its rental-license process, and its business-license guidance addresses separate locations. Use the property’s actual location to identify the applicable licensing file rather than assuming a neighboring beach community follows the same process. Gulf Shores rental-license information.

Define the property study

Owners with more than one beach rental should reconcile furniture and improvements by property. A bulk invoice for mattresses, kitchenware, and patio furniture may serve several units. Mark the destination of each item and document transfers between properties. The study team should not have to infer which unit received an asset from a management-company charge alone.

Records that resolve the main questions

  • A property-by-property index of closing and licensing records
  • Bulk purchase invoices allocated to actual receiving properties
  • Transfer and replacement log for furnishings moved between rentals

A scoping example

An owner orders furniture for two condos but pays with one business card. Create separate inventory rows with a property identifier, quantity, and invoice reference. Have the bookkeeper reconcile the split to the total payment before giving either condo file to the study provider.

Illustrative documentation scenario; not a completed client study or a savings estimate.

Can one study cover several Gulf Shores rentals?

Discuss the engagement scope, but keep each property’s basis, service dates, and supporting schedules distinguishable. A portfolio-level fee should not erase the audit trail for the assets allocated to each address.
